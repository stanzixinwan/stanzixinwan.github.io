In the Van Hooser Lab at Brandeis University I built research software for large-scale 2-photon calcium imaging and wrote an honors thesis on functional decoding in visual cortex.

Highlights
======
- Transformed terabytes of raw fluorescence streams into memory-mapped binary representations.
- Wrote 50+ modular feature-extraction scripts, cutting processing time by about 40%.
- Maintained CI/CD for open-source tools (`vhlab-toolbox` and NDI-matlab).
- Defended an honors thesis on neural tuning dynamics; manuscript in preparation.

This project is the scientific-computing side of my work: making large neural datasets cheaper to store, faster to analyze, and easier to share.
