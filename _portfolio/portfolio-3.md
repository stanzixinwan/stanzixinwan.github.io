Van Hooser Lab, Brandeis University (advisor: Stephen D. Van Hooser). Research software for 2-photon calcium imaging, plus an honors thesis on functional decoding in visual cortex.

Highlights
======
- Memory-mapped binaries from terabytes of raw fluorescence.
- 50+ feature-extraction scripts; about 40% faster processing.
- CI for open-source tools (`vhlab-toolbox`, NDI-matlab).
- Honors thesis on neural tuning dynamics; manuscript in preparation.
