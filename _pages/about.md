I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at Columbia University. I recently finished a B.S. in Computer Science and Biology at Brandeis University (*magna cum laude*). I work where machine learning, robotics, and research software meet: turning messy multimodal data into pipelines, training loops, and tools other people can run.

In 2026 I was a visiting student researcher at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, building high-throughput data pipelines and scaling vision-language-action training. Before that I spent two years in the Van Hooser Lab, where I wrote an honors thesis on functional decoding in visual cortex and built compute workflows for terabyte-scale 2-photon imaging.

I am looking for internships and research collaborations. If you care about ML systems, robotic manipulation, or computational neuroscience, the shortest paths are the [Research](/research/) page, the [Portfolio](/portfolio/), and the [CV](/cv/) ([PDF](/files/Resume_Zixin_Wan.pdf)).
