I work on **neural data analysis** and **robot learning systems**: decoding visual cortex activity at scale, and training vision-language-action policies from multimodal robot demonstrations.

Honors thesis
======
**Functional decoding and neural tuning dynamics in visual cortex**  
Brandeis University, 2024–2026  
Advisor: [Stephen D. Van Hooser](https://www.brandeis.edu/biology/faculty/van-hooser-stephen.html) ([lab](https://www.vhlab.org/))

I wrote and defended a senior honors thesis on how visual cortical neurons encode and change their tuning. Alongside the thesis I built the lab software that made the analysis possible: Python/MATLAB pipelines that turn terabytes of 2-photon calcium imaging into memory-mapped binaries, plus 50+ feature-extraction scripts (about 40% faster processing). I also maintained CI for open-source lab tools (`vhlab-toolbox`, NDI-matlab).

**Status:** manuscript in preparation.

Vision-language-action and manipulation
======
**Visiting student researcher**  
Shanghai Jiao Tong University, June–Sept 2026  
Advisor: [Yutong Ban](https://sirius.sjtu.edu.cn/people.html)

I engineered a multimodal data pipeline that synchronizes dual-arm joint states with 3-channel RGB at 30 FPS (35% lower I/O latency across 100+ episodes), scaled SFT and advantage-conditioned training for an InternVL-1B VLA model on 8×A100 GPUs with PyTorch FSDP, and improved cross-task policy success by 18%. I also built a human-in-the-loop teleoperation stack for closed-loop evaluation.
